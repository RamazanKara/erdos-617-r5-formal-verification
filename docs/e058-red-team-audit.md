# E058 internal red-team audit

Date: 2026-07-24 (Europe/Berlin)

Status: internal AI-assisted adversarial review of the local release candidate.
This is not independent expert review. Ramazan Kara has not yet approved
remote publication.

## Scope

The review targeted the exact chain from the 89 E038/E042/E043/E045
certificate leaves to

```lean
Erdos617.e058Problem617AtFive : Problem617At 5
```

It inspected the backward-core and stage generators, both external-checker
gates, primary-unit provenance, generated Lean sources, graph-semantic
closures, symmetry coverage, assumption inventory, clean-start/resume
provenance, evidence collection, deterministic packaging, preprint, and scope
language. Solver UNSAT returns and earlier session assertions were treated as
non-evidence.

## Findings and dispositions

1. **Clean-start metadata could overstate a resumed run.** The originally
   executed runner recorded `fresh_local_build=true` whenever the command-line
   gate was present, even on a later `--resume` invocation. The original
   receipt and runner are preserved unchanged. A separate sequence validator
   now requires the no-build gate's certificate-regeneration, 1,315-job build,
   and 89-core replay markers to precede the first resume marker, binds the
   sole terminal success marker to the raw receipt hash, and records every
   resume segment. The hardened final runner reports
   `fresh_local_build=false` on resume and embeds the validated clean-start
   sequence receipt.
   Bare audit-receipt JSON is no longer accepted as clean-start evidence: the
   hardened runner requires the complete sequence-provenance wrapper and
   requires both it and the bound raw receipt to record a post-core resume.
   The clean-start coverage source already contained and audited the
   unconditional `e058Problem617AtFive` declaration. The final source later
   added the explicit derived corollary `e058NoR5Counterexample` and replaced
   one redundant assumption query while keeping the total at 192. The
   hardened final phase recompiles all eight coverage modules and audits the
   final source inventory, but it is reported as a resume rather than as a
   second clean build.

2. **The legacy clean-start runner did not embed its own hash.** Rather than
   altering historical evidence, the sequence validator pins the exact
   executed runner at SHA-256
   `c69d27f7ca1cc5c98c087c287f832ca9d1ed4925d21f4166f762af1c0b37fb72`.
   The wrapper and final runner hash-check the archived runner, raw receipt,
   and transcript bytes.

3. **A completed kernel receipt originally permitted overly shallow reuse.**
   The hardened kernel-import runner now regenerates every stage split and
   every generated stage module in temporary directories, byte-compares them
   with the retained sources, and validates every source, object, log,
   assumption line, and receipt before a completed proof may be skipped.
   Tampering and incomplete-source cases are covered by the 25-test E058 unit
   target.

4. **The hardened semantic aggregate had an unreachable final-receipt block.**
   Static review found the result construction accidentally nested after an
   unconditional error raise. This did not affect the earlier clean-start
   runner, but it would have prevented the final hardened validation from
   completing. The indentation was corrected, and an AST-level regression
   assertion now requires the aggregate result assignment to remain in the
   direct body of `main`. Reuse of a completed semantic receipt now also
   revalidates its exact source, object, core, unit, kernel receipt, two logs,
   theorem metadata, and axiom lines; the existing 25-test target includes a
   retained-object tampering rejection.

5. **Generated-source and packaging dependencies were initially incomplete.**
   The release source inventory now includes every location-independent
   generated kernel and semantic `.lean` file, all E058 scripts, and the
   complete small Python support tree used by those scripts. The audit
   regenerates the generated sources byte-for-byte, while the evidence
   collector records their individual and combined hashes. Archive member
   modes are derived from file content (shebang scripts `0755`, all other
   files `0644`) so inherited Windows/WSL executable bits cannot change the
   payload.

6. **Primary branch assumptions required an independent provenance link.**
   The committed unit manifest is regenerated by streaming each exact
   compressed source CNF, checking its raw hash and dimensions, and extracting
   only primary edge-unit clauses. The audit compares all 89 generated and
   committed unit records and then checks their source hashes against the
   independently regenerated core receipts. Coverage theorems prove the
   corresponding signed edge literals from normalized graph hypotheses. The
   hardened resume additionally re-streams all 89 pinned source formulas into
   a temporary unit extraction and compares it with both the retained audit
   manifest and the committed unit files before any semantic receipt is
   accepted.

7. **The preregistered branch-02 benchmark lacked a repository artifact and
   its first dedicated runner was not replay-ready.** Historical timing text
   was not accepted. Audit execution exposed a stale additions-only proof
   hash after deterministic deletion records had been added, an ASCII writer
   for Unicode Lean source, an out-of-package source path rejected by Lake,
   resource measurement outside the process that actually ran Lean, and a
   cleanup indentation error that masked the timeout diagnostic. The final
   runner pins the full reduced proof and map, writes UTF-8, uses portable
   in-package relative paths, measures Lean directly, and cleans up after both
   imports.

   The one correctly measured raw attempt did not meet the preregistered
   performance cap: timeout exit 124, 310.08 user seconds, 7.57 system
   seconds, 5:19.34 elapsed including termination grace and cleanup, and
   5,924,372 KiB maximum RSS. The raw source and logs are retained. The
   reduced comparison side was therefore not run and no speedup ratio or
   successful benchmark receipt is claimed. This is a disclosed failure of a
   diagnostic reproducibility gate, not a Lean-kernel proof failure: the same
   reduced core is independently regenerated, dual-checked, staged, and
   accepted in every successful theorem audit.

8. **Scope and attribution could be mistaken for a general resolution.**
   The README, result record, release notes, paper, and AI disclosure must all
   say that only fixed \(r=5\) is machine verified. Robert Sneiderman is
   credited for the original mathematical proof. The claimed fixed cases
   \(r=6,\ldots,9\) remain unverified here, the all-\(r\) conjecture remains
   open, and no independent expert review is claimed.

9. **The first hardened final replay checked the theorem but crashed while
   writing its receipt.** Static assignment was missing for the validated
   kernel and semantic aggregate documents, so final JSON construction raised
   `NameError` after all Lean assumption output had completed. The failed log
   and driver are preserved. Both aggregate-return assignments were restored,
   the focused unit suite passed, and a new hardened replay repeated unit
   extraction, all receipt/source/object validation, all eight coverage
   compilations, and all 192 assumption queries before exiting zero with
   receipt SHA-256
   `7aa6cf6d38d3a38f1fa42867d15c1ebd48a8d6e191a35e69c37aff0fac6c0326`.

10. **The exact committed final source initially lacked one uninterrupted
    build-free replay.** The first exact-source attempt was terminated by the
    command-session boundary while Lean was compiling the cross-pattern
    certificate. The second successfully compiled every theorem-bearing Lean
    library target but stopped before core replay because the fresh mirror did
    not contain the separately compiled C checker required by the documented
    workflow. Neither attempt wrote a terminal receipt, and both are retained
    as explicitly labeled infrastructure diagnostics.

    A third mirror was extracted from exact commit
    `d19a0cf786a0fa714289830f276cf406408ab65b`; the C checker was compiled
    from the committed upstream source before launch, and
    `formal/lean/.lake/build` was absent. One uninterrupted audit then
    regenerated the transparent certificates, rebuilt all theorem-bearing
    Lean targets, replayed all 89 cores through both checkers and 356
    corruption tests, freshly imported all 89 kernel proofs, built all 89
    semantic closures and eight coverage modules, and ran all 192 assumption
    queries. It exited zero after 25,497.97 seconds. Its receipt SHA-256 is
    `789acb2bc829fcba0a5656fabca5a37d9d4fdd2b493858039ad0081cc37032d1`
    and explicitly records `fresh_local_build=true`,
    `resumed_after_core_replay=false`, zero warnings, and zero forbidden
    source hits.

## Residual limitations

- The earlier successful sequence began with a genuinely build-free source
  tree but used post-core resume segments; that exact history remains part of
  the evidence. The later exact-HEAD audit removes this qualification for the
  final committed source, but it remains a roughly seven-hour, high-memory
  reproduction on the recorded machine rather than independent expert review.
- The practical trusted computing base still includes the Lean parser,
  elaborator, kernel binary, operating system, and hardware. External
  checkers, Python generators, receipts, and hashes are reproducibility and
  defense-in-depth checks, not theorem oracles.
- No qualified independent human has yet reviewed the formal semantics,
  generated proof terms, or preprint.
- Nothing in E058 verifies fixed \(r=6,\ldots,9\) or proves the quantified
  all-\(r\) conjecture.

The local commit is therefore a review candidate only. No remote push, tag, or
release is authorized until Ramazan Kara has cross-checked it and explicitly
approved publication.
